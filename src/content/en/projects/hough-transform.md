This experiment applies a classic computer vision pipeline: grayscale
conversion, edge extraction, and line detection with the Hough Transform.

Different types of images—including buildings, bridges, structures, and
notebook pages—were used to observe how noise, perspective, and contrast change
the final result.

The repository preserves the original images, intermediate versions, and final
results, making the process visually auditable.
