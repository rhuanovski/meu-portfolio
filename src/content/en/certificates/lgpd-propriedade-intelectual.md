I attended the short course **Brazilian General Data Protection Law with an Emphasis on Intellectual Property**, held on **October 3, 2023**, with a total workload of **3 hours**.

The activity was promoted by **PROFNIT at the Federal University of Southern and Southeastern Pará** and conducted by **Prof. Dr. Cláudio Henrique Cerqueira Costa Basquerotto** and **MSc. Sara Brigida Farias Ferreira**.

The course addressed the relationship between personal data protection, Brazil's General Data Protection Law, and intellectual property.
