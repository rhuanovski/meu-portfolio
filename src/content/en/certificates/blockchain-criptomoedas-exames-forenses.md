I attended **Blockchain and Cryptocurrencies: Concepts and Forensic Examinations**, a lecture held during the inaugural class of Unifesspa's Graduate Program in Forensic Sciences.

The **2-hour** activity took place on **March 28, 2025**, and was presented by **Brazilian Federal Criminal Expert Pedro Monteiro Eleutério**.

The lecture introduced key concepts in blockchain and cryptocurrencies from the perspective of investigations and forensic examinations.
