I participated as an **attendee** in the **III Academic Week of Computer Engineering**, held between **November 13 and 15, 2024**, with a total workload of **20 hours**.

## About the event

The Academic Week of Computer Engineering is a space for integration among students, professors, researchers, and professionals in the technology field.

During the event, I had exposure to lectures, discussions, and experiences related to academic training, the job market, and the different possibilities of working within Computer Engineering.

## Importance for my development

The participation helped broaden my perspective on the field, allowed me to learn about different professional experiences, and enabled me to follow discussions on technology, innovation, and the development of computational solutions.

In addition to the technical knowledge shared, the event also provided interaction with the academic community and contact with other students and professionals interested in computing and engineering.

This experience complemented my university education and reinforced the importance of attending events that bridge the academic environment with the transformations of the technology sector.
